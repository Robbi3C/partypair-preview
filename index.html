Asked in every group chat

How much does it cost?

Every weekend is custom, so pricing depends on your house, your headcount and how far we take the design. You'll get an exact quote after a free design chat — before you commit to anything. Décor, delivery, setup and breakdown are always included.

How far ahead should we book?

Cape Cod summer weekends go quickly. If the group chat has picked a date, check it early — it's free and takes two minutes.

Our rental says no tape, no nails, no glitter. Problem?

That's our specialty. Everything we use is damage-free and rental-safe, and we break it all down after checkout so the house goes back exactly how we found it. Your deposit is safe with us.

Do we need to be there when you set up?

Nope — that's the whole point. We coordinate with your rental's check-in window and finish before the group arrives, so the walk-in moment stays a surprise. Even for you, if you want it that way.

Where do you go?

All of Cape Cod — Falmouth to Provincetown — and beyond. Staying somewhere unusual? Ask. We love a weird house.

Do you do more than décor?

Yes — we're a concierge at heart. Fridge stocking, gift-bag assembly, itinerary help, welcome carts, s'mores nights, hangover baskets… and if it's not on the list, just ask. Tell us the dream on your design chat and we'll tell you what's possible.
