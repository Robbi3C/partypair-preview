Questions, answered

How much does a bachelorette party setup cost?

Every setup is custom, so pricing depends on your house, your headcount, and how far we take the design. Most bookings land between $750 and $2,000 all-in — design, sourcing, delivery, setup, and breakdown included. You'll get an exact quote after your free design call, before you commit to anything.

How far in advance should we book?

We take one party per weekend, and summer weekends (May–September) usually sell out by early spring. If you have a date, check it now — checking costs nothing and takes two minutes.

Our rental has strict rules — no tape, no nails, no glitter. Can you still work with it?

That's our specialty. Everything we use is damage-free and rental-safe: freestanding structures, removable fixings, zero-residue methods. We've never cost a group their security deposit, and we break everything down so the house is checkout-ready.

What areas do you serve?

All of Cape Cod, Massachusetts — from Bourne and Falmouth out to Provincetown, including Sandwich, Barnstable, Hyannis, Yarmouth, Dennis, Harwich, Chatham, Brewster, Orleans, Eastham, Wellfleet and Truro. Staying somewhere unusual? Ask — we love a weird house.

Do we need to be there when you set up?

Nope — that's the whole point. We coordinate directly with your rental's check-in window and finish before the group arrives. You walk into the finished house together, and the walk-in moment stays a surprise for everyone (including you, if you want it that way).

Do you do anything beyond decor?

Yes — we're a concierge at heart. Fridge and bar stocking, welcome bags, custom merch, picnic setups, and local recommendations for dinners and activities are all things we can layer on. Tell us the dream on your design call and we'll tell you what's possible.
