Asked in every group chat

How much does it cost?

Every weekend is custom, so it depends on your house, your headcount and how far we take it. You'll get an exact quote after a free design chat — before you commit to anything. Décor, delivery, setup and breakdown are always included.

How far ahead should we book?

We take one party per weekend, and summer dates go by spring. If the group chat has picked a date, check it now — it's free and it takes two minutes.

Our Airbnb says no tape, no nails, no glitter. Problem?

That's our specialty. Everything is damage-free and rental-safe, and we break it all down after checkout so the house goes back exactly how we found it. Your deposit is safe with us.

Do we need to be there when you set up?

Nope — that's the whole point. We work with your rental's check-in window and finish before anyone arrives, so the walk-in moment stays a surprise. Even for you, if you want it that way.

Where do you go?

All of Cape Cod — Falmouth to Provincetown — & beyond. Staying somewhere unusual? Ask. We love a weird house.
